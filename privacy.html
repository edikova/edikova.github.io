Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Collective Automation is a private Google Apps Script project used only by its owner.

What it accesses

It reads and writes Google Sheets and Docs in the owner's own Google Drive. It does not access any other person's Google account.

What it shares

To research companies, it sends public company names and websites to a web research service. It never sends personal information about any person. It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone else.

Storage

All data stays in the owner's Google Drive. Settings are kept in the script's own properties inside Google Apps Script.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
